## Evidence boundary

No current raw verdict is committed. The historical output was generated by
<code>repro/src/verify_mwgrad.py</code>, a superseded five-dimensional
toy/proxy suite, not by the root <code>verify_all.py</code> implementation. It
recorded failures for the strong-convexity and acceleration-consistency
checks and used a synthetic proxy for the paper's Bayesian multi-task
experiment.

The source paths and their limitations are documented in the root README.
Generated evidence may be accepted only after a fresh run records the source
commit, environment, parameters, data provenance, raw outputs, and an
independent checker.
